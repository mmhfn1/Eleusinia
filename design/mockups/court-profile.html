Banner, play and theme

A companion to court.html for items 1 to 3 of the next build. Every colour and typeface here is court's; only the pieces are new.

1. A server's banner

The column banner: 172 px, edge to edge of the column, the name in Bodoni at 22 px on two lines at most with the member count under it. Its last 56 px fade into the column's own background, so there is no edge; the channel list starts below it and scrolls up under the fade. Left at the smallest window (900 by 600, the column at its 300 px floor) with a long name and fourteen channels; right, the list scrolled, a one-line name and a very bright banner.

Scrolled, short name, bright banner

2. Two weeks of play, under the status

Fourteen days in a row, oldest on the left, today ringed. Each cell is the profile's own accent at four strengths by hours played that day (under 1 h, 1 to 2, 2 to 4, 4 or more), a fixed scale so a friend's grid means the same as yours; an empty day is a stone tint, not the accent. No key under the cells: each one's label says it. Hover or Tab to a cell: the date, then each game and its hours. The same grid on the small card, the full card, yours and others'. Recently played, on the full card only: the last five games, newest first, with when each was last played; its own record, so a game from three weeks ago still shows. Below: fewer than five, then the empty states.

3. A profile theme

Settings, My profile, Profile theme. Pick one of the four weaves (or none) and turn its tones; Strength sets how much of it shows behind the card. Motion: Off, Drift (the weave slides slowly) or Breathe (it fades in and out); Speed and Depth set how fast and how far. The small card on the right is the live preview, exactly what friends see; it plays here while the controls are touched and stops a few seconds after. Text always sits on a paper scrim that grows with Strength, so it keeps AA contrast. Reduced motion holds it still, and it pauses off screen or in a hidden window.
