Voice: picture in picture, the voice panel, right-click menus, invite cards

A static mockup for approval. It loads the app's own src/renderer/style.css; everything that exists today (rail, server column, chat, members, the right-click menu with its checkbox items and unfolding items, the per-person volume slider, the connection icon, the stage's buttons, the link preview card) uses its existing classes unchanged. Only what is new is added, in the last part of this file's style block. Every frame is 1280 by 800.

1 · Picture in picture: Juno's stream follows you into #general

You were watching Juno's stream in Raid and opened #general. The stream keeps playing (with sound) in a small player in the bottom right of the chat, above the composer, over the messages. Drag it by any part to another corner; the grip only hints that it moves. Its controls show on hover (shown here): Back to call (opens Raid with the stream big again), mute the stream, Leave stream (stops watching, you stay in the call), Expand (full window). The caption, the live dot, the dark buttons and the volume are the stage's own.

2 · The voice bar and the user bar, in the server you are talking in

Two bars across the bottom of the rail and the left column together. The user bar, always there: your picture with your status dot (a click opens your profile), name, "In voice" while in a call; mute and deafen, each with a small arrow for its device menu, and the gear (Settings, which leaves the rail). Above it, only while in a call, the voice bar, in place of the current "Voice connected" block: the connection icon (the existing one, in the good colour; hover shows the pings, as today), Voice connected, the channel and server, noise suppression (lit while on) and Disconnect; under it camera and share screen. The rail now ends above the bars, with only + at its foot.

4 · The arrow beside mute: microphone and input volume

A click on the small arrow beside mute opens the existing menu above it: the microphones (a check on the one in use, a click switches at once), the input volume (the per-person volume slider, reused), and Voice settings, which opens Settings at Voice. The arrow beside deafen does the same for speakers and output volume.

8 · Right-click Theo in Raid (you are an Officer, Theo a Member)

Profile, Message, Start a call. Then what only changes things for you: his volume (the existing slider), and three checkboxes in the member menu checkbox style: Mute (his voice stops playing for you), Disable video (his camera and screen stop coming to you), Ignore (only silences notifications from his DMs). Then Invite to server, unfolded: your servers Theo is not in; a click sends him an invite link in your DM. Add friend, Block. Last, because you outrank him and have the permissions (servers only): Server mute and Server deafen, then Move to (unfolds the other voice channels: Lounge) and Disconnect.

An invite link in any chat shows as a card built from the link preview card: the server's picture (or its letter tile), "Server invite", the name, members and how many are online, and one button. Join joins in one click; Open for a server you are already in (your own invite to Ashen Vanguard); an expired one says so and its button is off (the older Iron Lotus invite). The link text itself is not repeated above the card.
